**The code is free. The name is not.**

covers copyright: you may use, study, modify and redistribute the software, including commercially,

It does not grant any right to the **dika studio** name, logos, wordmarks or visual identity. Those
are held by Dika Design Ltd and are not part of the licence grant. This is the normal arrangement
for source-available projects and it exists for one reason: so that somebody downloading
something called dika studio knows where it came from.

## What you may do without asking

- Use the software for anything, including in a business.
- Fork it, modify it, and publish your fork under the same licence.
- Say truthfully that your project is "based on dika studio Community Editor" or "a fork of dika studio
  Community Editor", in ordinary descriptive text.
- Keep the name and marks in an **unmodified** copy that you redistribute as-is.

## What needs a different name

If you distribute a **modified** version, give it your own name and your own icon. Specifically:

- Do not name it dika studio, or a name likely to be confused with it.
- Do not use the dika studio logos, wordmarks, icons or colour identity as your product's.
- Do not present your fork in a way that suggests it is published, endorsed or supported by
  Dika Design Ltd.

Replacing the branding is deliberately easy. The application name, icon and the upsell link live in
a small number of files, and nothing in the build depends on them.

## Why the software makes this easy on purpose

The build ships an "unbranded" path rather than hiding the branding behind obfuscation, because a
trademark policy that is technically painful to comply with is a policy people ignore. If you find a
place where rebranding is harder than it should be, that is a bug and we would like to hear about it.

## Questions

If you are unsure whether your use is covered, ask before you ship. A short question is cheaper for
both of us than a dispute afterwards.
